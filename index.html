Game dev and bug hunter — 7 years in, 2 games on Steam, 20+ projects in the log.

dkeiz@dev:~$ cat ./story.txt

I started on a Unity build pipeline at Gear Inc. in 2020, spent two years cleaning datasets and hunting bugs in a HUST lab, then went full game dev at GD Studio — gameplay systems, enemy AI and bullet-hell performance, through to level design and QA. Two of those games made it to Steam.

I like systems that are simple but durable: code a teammate can read, maintain and not curse at. Good architecture is the kind a newcomer understands in ten minutes — not the kind that needs a wiki.

Meanwhile I'm chasing a BEng in ICT at HUST (expected 2027) and picked up a BBA in 2026 — because shipping games is half engineering, half everything else.

Off the clock I keep studying — learning while shipping is how I level up fastest.
